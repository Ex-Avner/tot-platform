Research Consent & Data Notice

The TOT Assessment is part of ongoing research into Triaxial Orientation Theory, developed by Ross Erickson / Avner Media. This instrument is currently in its pre-validation phase. Results are exploratory and not a clinical diagnosis.

  • Your responses are stored anonymously. No name or email address is linked to your assessment data in research records.
  • Your anonymized orientation profile may be used in statistical analysis, academic research, and peer-reviewed publication.
  • If you choose to provide your email address, it will be used only to deliver your PDF report. It is not retained, linked to your results, or shared with any third party.
  • You may withdraw at any time by closing the browser. Incomplete sessions are not saved.
  • This instrument is not a substitute for clinical assessment or professional mental health services.
